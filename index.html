Our First Seven Weeks

Dear Turtle Bay Families,

Seven weeks into the school year, our students have settled into their routines with curiosity, kindness, and growing confidence. This issue shares what the Student Experience Department has been working on, and what your children have been part of, since the very first day.

Whole-School Belonging

The Community Program: Celebrating Our Values

This year we launched our Community Program, a new initiative to reinforce positive behavior and school values across the whole school. All students and staff have been distributed into four Communities (Stars, Dunes, Pearls, and Corals), so that children from different grades and classes belong to a shared team alongside teachers and staff members.

The Communities come together regularly to celebrate students who model our values in action. The program builds belonging, teamwork, and pride, and it recognizes the everyday choices that make Turtle Bay a caring and respectful place to learn.

Weekly Character & IB

Morning Assemblies: A Weekly Theme

Our morning assemblies are running every week, and each week centers on a theme. The theme is either an attribute of the IB Learner Profile (such as Caring, Principled, or Reflective) or a trait of the Global Competitive Citizen (HCDP). Classrooms carry the theme into discussions, activities, and recognition, helping students live these values day to day.

Themes Covered So Far (First 7 Weeks)

Being Disciplined Weekly Focus
Building self-control, focus, and positive habits across every learning space.

Wellbeing Special

International Mental Health Day: Assembly with Dr. Maram Alsamman

To mark International Mental Health Day, we held a school assembly and were honored to host Dr. Maram Alsamman, who spoke with our students about mental health and wellbeing.

The assembly reinforced a message we share with students all year: People are different in many ways, but everyone is looking for mental health, safety, kindness, and hope.

"People are different in many ways, but everyone is looking for mental health, safety, kindness, and hope. "
— Dr. Maram Alsamman
Dr. Maram Alsamman speaking with students about mental health and wellbeing at the school assembly.
